How it works

1

Photograph before

Walk the property and snap a "before" photo of every surface — driveway, siding, roof, deck — and photograph any pre-existing damage before you touch anything. Thirty seconds now saves an argument later.

2

Do the work

Run the job checklist — arrived, water access, property protected, work completed, walkthrough, invoice sent — and note the service and condition for each surface.

3

Send the proof

Take the "after" photos and download a branded PDF report — before and after side by side, the service performed, pre-existing damage, and a clear documentation status: COMPLETE only when every surface has both photos and the checklist is done. Send it to the client or the property manager.

The fine print, in plain words

Privacy

Your job details and photos are stored only in your own browser on your own device — photos in the browser's local database, never uploaded to us or anyone else. There is no account and no tracking of your clients or photos. Photos take up real space on the device, so export a backup file from the app regularly; clearing your browser data erases what's saved here.

A word about photos: the photos you take belong to your cleaning business and to the property owner — they're the owner's home, after all. Get the owner's permission before photographing a property and before sharing reports that contain photos. Photograph the surfaces and the work — keep people, vehicles' plates, and personal belongings out of frame where you can. And because your browser is the only place photos live until you export them, download each job's evidence pack promptly and keep it in your own storage (Drive, Dropbox, or similar) — we never hold a copy, so what's lost from the device is lost for good.

Billing & cancellation

Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt or the Stripe customer portal — no email to us required, no cancellation fee. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the free job builder keeps working either way.
